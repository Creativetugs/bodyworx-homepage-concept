WELCOME TO BODYWORX

A gym where you belong.

Mark and Susan Pressley opened BodyWorx in 1997 with a simple goal: provide Oxford with a bright, clean, well-maintained fitness center and a team that cares about its members.

Whether you are starting a new routine, keeping active, building strength, or joining a class, we want you to feel comfortable asking questions and working at your own pace.
